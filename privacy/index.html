Privacy Policy

How Fastoolee handles tool inputs and basic website data.

Tool data

QR generation is designed to run client-side where applicable. Fastoolee does not intentionally upload URLs, Wi‑Fi passwords, contact details or generated QR content to a server for these tools.

The contact form prepares an email or WhatsApp message on your device. Sending it is handled by your chosen email client or WhatsApp.

Analytics and advertising

Third-party analytics or advertising may be added separately in the future. If enabled, this policy will be updated to describe the relevant providers, cookies or identifiers.

Security and updates

No website can promise absolute security. We may update these practices, tools and this policy as the service evolves.
