Convert Pixels to CM, MM or Inches

Convert in both directions instantly. No upload, no submit button and no signup.

✓ 100% Free✓ No Sign Up✓ Runs in your browser

The conversion formula

Pixels = inches × DPI. Inches = pixels ÷ DPI. Centimeters convert to inches by dividing by 2.54; millimeters divide by 25.4.

Why DPI is required

There is no single fixed number of pixels in a centimeter. The answer changes with the selected DPI.

Useful presets

Use 72 or 96 DPI for common screen calculations and 300 DPI for many print workflows. Always follow the requirement of the platform or printer you are using.

Privacy & processing

The selected file is processed in your browser and is not uploaded to Fastoolee.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert pixels to cm?

Divide pixels by DPI to get inches, then multiply by 2.54.

Can I convert cm back to pixels?

Yes. Switch direction, enter the physical value and DPI, and the result updates instantly.

Why do I need DPI?

Because physical units do not have a fixed pixel equivalent without a density.
