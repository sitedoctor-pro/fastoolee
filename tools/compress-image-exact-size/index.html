    Exact limit vs normal compression

    “Low / medium / high” compression does not solve a 100 KB upload requirement. This tool iterates encoding quality and can reduce dimensions when you explicitly allow it.

    Target size is not magic

    Different images compress differently. A photo may reach 50 KB easily while a detailed screenshot may need smaller dimensions. Fastoolee shows the achieved size instead of claiming false precision.

    JPG, PNG or WebP

    JPG and WebP support quality-based compression. PNG is lossless, so strict small-size targets may require dimension reduction or a different output format.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I compress an image under 100 KB?

    Yes. Set Maximum size to 100 KB. The tool adjusts quality and can reduce dimensions if allowed.

    Why is the result 92 KB instead of exactly 100 KB?

    Image encoders do not provide exact byte control. Staying under the limit is usually the important requirement.

    Does compression reduce resolution?

    Quality compression can preserve dimensions. If the target is too small, dimension reduction may be needed.
