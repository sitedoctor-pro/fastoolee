About Fastoolee

Fastoolee is a lightweight collection of free online tools built for speed, privacy and practical everyday use.

What we build

The launch toolkit focuses on QR creation and scanning. The same brand can later expand into image, PDF, developer and utility tools without changing the privacy-first approach.

How it works

Fastoolee is a static website. QR generation that can run locally is processed in your browser, reducing unnecessary data transfer and keeping the interface fast.

Our priorities

Useful tools first, clear interfaces, accessibility, mobile performance and honest privacy communication.

Fast, free online tools for exact image, PDF and QR tasks. No sign up.
